## Tips for Better Response Rates:

1. **Personalize** - Mention something specific about the buyer
2. **Keep it short** - No one reads 500-word emails
3. **Show value fast** - Lead with the demo link
4. **Follow up** - 70% of responses come after 2nd+ touch
5. **Be available** - Respond within 1 hour of inquiry
