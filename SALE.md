## 🎯 Product Overview

DealPilot AI is an AI-powered SaaS for real estate wholesalers and investors. It helps users manage motivated seller leads, generate AI-powered outreach messages, automate follow-ups, and organize deals in one dashboard.

### Problem It Solves
Real estate wholesalers waste hours daily on:
- Manual lead management
- Crafting personalized outreach
- Tracking deal progress
- Following up with leads

### Solution
All-in-one platform with:
- AI-generated SMS, cold call scripts, and voicemail scripts
- Visual Kanban pipeline for deal tracking
- Lead CRM with search/filter/bulk actions
- Automated follow-up reminders
- Dashboard analytics
